# Angelia brand assets

- `angelia-mark.svg`: the mark on a transparent background
- `angelia-logo.svg`, `angelia-logo-500.png`, `angelia-logo-1024.png`: the mark on the brand background (avatars, social cards)

The mark is the **Alpha of Angelia** (ἀγγελία, "message, news", the messenger spirit and daughter of Hermes): a capital A whose crossbar is a **version-bump chevron**, with **message waves** rising from its apex like a wing. Detect, normalize, deliver.

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night | `#15132B` | background |
| Parchment | `#F3EEE4` | letterform |
| Gold | `#E9B14C` | chevron and waves |

The palette is shared with the sibling project [Horae](https://github.com/horae-dev).

PNGs are rendered from the SVG with headless Chrome (ImageMagick's built-in SVG renderer drops strokes).

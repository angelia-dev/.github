# Angelia brand assets

- `angelia-logo.svg`, `angelia-logo-500.png`, `angelia-logo-1024.png`: the logo with rounded corners (transparent outside), for READMEs, docs and social cards
- `angelia-mark.svg`, `angelia-avatar-1024.png`: the same mark full bleed (square), for avatars

## Meaning

The **A of Angelia** (ἀγγελία, "message, news"; the messenger, daughter of Hermes) drawn as a rising peak. Its crossbar is a **herald's wing** of three gold feathers sweeping up and out: a message taking off. The **spark** at the apex is the new release.
